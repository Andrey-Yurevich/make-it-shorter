## Notes on the "Summarization services in top-10" column

Source: Semrush `phrase_organic`, database `us`, top 10 organic positions, pulled per keyword individually.

Counting rule (my own classification of the Semrush results, not a Semrush metric):
a result counts when the **ranking page itself is a text summarizing / shortening / condensing
tool** (or that tool's app-store listing). Distinct services only — a domain appearing twice,
or a site plus its own Play Store listing, counts once.

Not counted:
- URL shorteners (`shorturl.at`, `tinyurl.com`, `bitly.com`, `unshorten.it`)
- video "Shorts" makers (`shortmake.com` — verified, `short.ai`, `opus.pro`, `canva.com/create/short-videos`, `wayin.ai`, `vmaker.com`)
- dictionaries and thesauri (Merriam-Webster, Cambridge, Collins, Wiktionary, Vocabulary.com)
- UGC and video (Reddit, Quora, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Stack Exchange)
- guides and blog posts, **including those hosted on tool domains** (e.g. `originality.ai/blog/sentence-shortener`, `blog.outwrite.com`)
- crossword solvers, `ludwig.guru` (sentence search, not a shortener)
- book-summary content services (`summaries.com`, `summary.com`, `shortform.com`, `jamesclear.com`) — they sell summaries, they don't summarize your text
- adjacent writing tools that expand or paraphrase rather than shorten (`paragraph-generator.com`, `ryrob.com/paragraph-expander`, `grammarly.com/.../sentence-rewriter`)

Two judgment calls worth flagging:
- **`summarization` (5)** includes Azure AI Language and Google Cloud summarization — API services, not consumer tools. Drop them and the count is 3.
- **`summerize` (3)** includes `summerise.netlify.app`, whose page renders client-side and could not be verified. Drop it and the count is 2.
