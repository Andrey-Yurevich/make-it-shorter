## Blind read
- Short description: fixed an ambiguous pronoun ("ho": the sentence or the text?) by putting "shorten and summarize" side by side. Kept "paste" and "one click", which the reader's version had dropped.
- "more to read than time" (calque) → "has more to read than they can get through".
- Lists and tables: "a list is again a list" → "a list remains a list". "Dates": "data" is ambiguous in Czech (dates or data), so it became "kalendářní data" (the reader's "datumy" is colloquial).
- "what stands in the source" (Germanism) → "what is in the original text". "Every point" of a paragraph → "all the information".
- Use cases: dropped the repetition "shorten really short" and "brief and to the point". "First reading of the reading list" → "first going through the recommended literature". "Keep" → "pick".
- "pastes neatly" (calque) → "you paste it smoothly". The reader suggested "without broken formatting", which was not taken because it claims more than the source does.
- "deserves twenty minutes" → "is worth twenty minutes of your time"; "go back to the source" → "check the original"; "AI is at work here, a nuance may escape" → "an AI does the summary, so it may occasionally miss a detail".
- "Press Shorten" → "click Shorten" everywhere, the usual verb for an on-screen button.
- "daily limit on requests" (technical) → "daily limit on the number of shortenings".
- FAQ: fixed an ungrammatical "even shorter?", rephrased "each round" (keeping the source's "can"), "your choice stays saved", "reads words, not pictures of them" and the "shorter please" line. The summary generator question became "Will it summarize my own drafts too?". The reader's version of the chat-message answer was not taken because it dropped "very short".
- The misspelling question (summerize/summerizer) was adapted to Czech searches typed without diacritics ("shrnuti textu"). The reader flagged it as SEO bait no Czech page would carry, so it was dropped. That leaves 9 Q&A pairs instead of 10.
- Closing line: dropped the repeated "shorten… shorten" and fixed the dangling "celé".
