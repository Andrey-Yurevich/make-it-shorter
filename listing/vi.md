# Vietnamese (vi)

## Name
Tóm tắt văn bản — Summarize text
Priority market. Semrush, database `vn`: `make it shorter` 40/month; best national phrasing "tóm tắt văn bản" 5,400 (×10 = 54,000, ~1,350× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. Sentence case, because Vietnamese capitalizes only the first syllable of a common phrase; Title Case on every syllable reads as a heading, not a name. The bare word "tóm tắt" (9,900) is broader ("summary" of anything) and not a name. Secondary keywords: "rút gọn văn bản" (880, "shorten text", the closest to what the extension does) and "tóm tắt nội dung" (720, "summarize the content"), each used once or twice. The "web …" variants (480, 320) describe a website and were left out. Phrasings with "AI" in them have no volume in this database. The name is now on the toolbar icon, so the setup step says "the extension's icon".
The misspelling FAQ uses unaccented typing, "tom tat van ban" (70; "tom tat" 140), which is very common in Vietnamese.

## Short description
Tóm tắt văn bản bằng AI chỉ với một cú nhấp. Dán câu ngắn hay cả bài dài vào là rút gọn được ngay, hỗ trợ mọi ngôn ngữ.
Summarize text with AI in just one click. Paste in a short sentence or a whole long article and it is shortened right away, in any language.
