## Blind read
- Short description: "get a result" (reads as "draw a conclusion") → "receive it".
- Opening: word-for-word "more to read than time" → idiomatic "lots to read, little time"; "a lonely paragraph" → "just one paragraph"; stiff "without any addition" → "without adding anything to it".
- "Is downloaded" (بارگیری) for loading the page into the panel → "the page text appears in the panel", in steps, the reload sentence and the first FAQ.
- "Watch the result being written" → "see the result written word by word".
- "Makes nothing up" line and "two settings you choose once" heading reworded idiomatically; "whatever language the page is in" → "no matter what language the page is in".
- Seminar → class (in Iran "seminar" means a graduate presentation).
- Coined "message thread" → "email chain"; the research line no longer reads like a string of keywords.
- Copy, "a summary tells you whether…", contract detail and "there is AI underneath" sentences rewritten in native word order.
- Daily limit sentence simplified; Academy word for "characters" → the everyday کاراکتر.
- Casual/Direct answer now says "press Shorten once more"; language-memory answer active instead of passive; "not pictures of them" → "not text inside images"; "make it shorter please" answer made a natural sentence; a draft is "any other text", not "any other page".
- Misspelling FAQ: Persian has no common misspelling of "summarize"; a synonym variant (تلخیص, "abridgement") was tried and the reader flagged it as obvious SEO padding, so the question was dropped.
- Closing sentence reordered Persian-style, the two "very short" variants kept in one clause.
- Kept the source meaning over the reader's version: in the work line the shortened text is still the email chain being forwarded (not "your own message"), and the research line still names AI papers.
- Kept despite the suggestion: پانل (the spelling Chrome uses for its side panel) instead of پنل.
