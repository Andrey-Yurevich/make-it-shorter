## Short description
Tehisintellekt lühendab lauseid ja artikleid ning teeb kokkuvõtteid. Kleebi sisse – ühe klõpsuga saad tulemuse mis tahes keeles.
AI shortens sentences and articles and makes summaries. Paste it in: one click gets you the result in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: "a sentence or a whole article" (singular, awkward) became "sentences and articles".
- Opening: "comes back as the same text" was a calque; now "you get it back as the same text, just shorter". Also fixed a doubled "add".
- "Reload the whole page" read like refreshing the browser; now "bring the whole page's text back into the panel".
- Reworded these to sound native: the short-paragraph sentence, "nothing made up", the research use ("sift through a reading list"), the copy line ("paste without losing formatting"), and the "worth twenty minutes" and contract-number sentences.
- Work use: removed "shorten it short" (said the same thing twice) and changed "correspondence" to "email thread".
- FAQ questions about "even shorter", "short and simple" and "short enough for a chat message" were fragments; they are now full sentences.
- "Not pictures of them" was ambiguous; now "not words captured in an image".
- Removed the misspelling FAQ. The candidate was "kokkuvote" without õ, and the reader said nobody would ask it because Estonian keyboards have õ.
- The closing sentence read like a keyword list; it now flows as one sentence, with the same meaning.
