# Brazilian Portuguese (pt_BR)

## Name
Resumir texto — Summarize text
Priority market. Semrush, database `br`: `make it shorter` 30/month; best national phrasing “resumir texto” 14,800 (×10 = 148,000, ~4,900× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. Sentence case, verb phrase, matching es/fr/it. Secondary keywords: “resumidor de texto” (8,100, "text summarizer") for "summarizer", “resumo de texto” / “resumo” for "summary", “gerador de resumo” (320) in the drafts question; plain "shorten" rendered with “reduzir”/“diminuir”/“enxugar” (reduzir/diminuir texto 210 each, “encurtar texto” 110). “Resoomer” (3,600) is a competitor brand, left out. Since the name is also the toolbar icon's title, step 1 points at the icon “Resumir texto”; elsewhere the product stays Make It Shorter.
The misspelling FAQ uses “rezumir”/“rezumidor” (70 and 110/month), the real s→z error.

Summarizing text with AI takes a single click: paste a sentence or the whole article and get it all back shorter, in whatever language you want.
