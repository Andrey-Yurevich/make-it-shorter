# Italian (it)

## Name
Riassumi testo — Summarize text
Priority market. Semrush, database `it`: `make it shorter` 20/month; best national phrasing «riassumi testo» 2,900 (×10 = 29,000, about 1,450× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone, in sentence case because Italian doesn't title-case. «riassunto online» (9,900) was passed over: its related queries are book and TV plot summaries and the civil-registry «estratto per riassunto», not tools that work on a given text. Secondary keywords: «riassunto AI» (1,300, so "AI" is used throughout rather than "IA"), «riassuntore» (online 720, AI 590; once, in the opening, where English has "ai summarizer"), «generatore di riassunti» (90). «accorciare un testo» ("shorten a text") gets 20 and is used only as an ordinary verb. The competitor brand «resoomer» and its misspellings are not used anywhere.
The name keyword appears in the short description in plural form («Riassumi testi», summarize texts), in step 1 of the full description («Fai clic sull'icona di Riassumi testo», because the toolbar icon's title is the name), and as «riassumere un testo» in the closing line. Wordstat-style national volumes are not available here, so all volumes are from Semrush.
The misspelling FAQ uses «riasunto» (720), the common one-s typo of «riassunto».

## Short description
Riassumi testi interi o accorcia una singola frase con l'AI, in qualsiasi lingua. Incolla un brano e basta un clic.
Summarize whole texts or shorten a single sentence with AI, in any language. Paste a passage and one click is enough.
