- Opening: fixed a broken "more to read, less time" construction; "comes back, but shorter" (a calque) → "shortens it and gives it to you"; "without mixing anything" → "without adding anything new".
- Steps: "the result gets written" (bookish passive) → "the result takes shape"; "bring the page again" → "load the page again".
- "Source text" is now one word throughout (అసలు టెక్స్ట్ instead of the stiff మూల టెక్స్ట్).
- The verb కుదురు (it means "to fit", not "to get shorter") is replaced with "becomes shorter / is shortened" everywhere.
- Settings heading moved to native word order ("Just two settings, choose once").
- Everyday uses: "this thread" → "the message thread"; the research item is now a full sentence; the lookup item reads "shorten five sources and keep the one with the answer".
- "Worth twenty minutes" paragraph: fixed the grammatical agreement; "there is AI underneath" (a calque) → "since it works with AI".
- Limits: "a limit on requests" (bureaucratic) → "a limited number of uses per day"; "Chrome's own pages" → "Chrome's internal pages"; "copy by hand" → "copy it yourself".
- FAQ questions now ask "can it be shortened?" instead of addressing a person.
