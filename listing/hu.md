## Short description
AI-jal rövidít és foglal össze, egyetlen mondattól a teljes szövegig. Illeszd be, és egy kattintással kész, bármilyen nyelven.
Shortens and summarizes with AI, from a single sentence to a whole text. Paste it in, and it's done in one click, in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: dropped the awkward "is" ("even") construction; now "from a single sentence to a whole text".
- Intro: "nothing gets in that wasn't there" now uses "benne" (in it) instead of the calqued "ott" (there).
- Step 4: the calque "watch the result being born" became "and you can read the result as it's being made".
- "Nothing made up": the slangy "Nincs kitaláció" became "It invents nothing: only what is also in the source".
- "How much shorter" paragraph: case and subject fixes ("the main ideas remain", "a short paragraph keeps every point, only the extra words disappear").
- Work use: "shorten it to concise and to the point" collapsed into one verb, "condense it to the point".
- Research use: the tacked-on AI papers clause became "even for papers about artificial intelligence".
- Copy line: the vague "can be pasted in an orderly way" became "pastes nicely into a document, an email or a chat too". The reader's "formatting is kept" was not taken: it claims more than the English.
- Daily limit: the bureaucratic "a daily limit applies to the number of requests" became "a limited number of requests a day per device".
- FAQ: "one press of Shorten" became "one click on Shorten"; "each pass can make it much shorter, until only the key points remain"; the screenshots answer no longer says "pictures of them" (which pointed at the words); "not a retelling" became "doesn't rephrase it"; "please" changed from slangy "légyszi" to "légy szíves".
- Misspelling question: kept as the Hungarian habit of searching without accents ("osszefoglalo"), reworded as "I searched without accents. Am I in the right place?". The reader called it SEO-like; it stays, since the English line is the same kind of line.
- Closing line: the elliptical "as short as possible" clause was merged into one sentence with a dash.
- Not taken: switching the FAQ questions away from addressing the tool as "you"; the reader said it works.
