# Spanish (es)

## Name
Resumidor de textos — Text summarizer
Priority market. Semrush, database `es`: `make it shorter` 20/month; best national phrasing «resumidor de textos» 22,200 (×10 = 222,000, ~10,000× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. The plural beats the singular «resumidor de texto» (1,300). The bare «resumidor» (12,100) is too broad to be a name. Secondary keywords: «resumir textos» (3,600), «resumen IA» (1,000), «generador de resúmenes» (170, the "summary generator" in the drafts question) and «hazme un resumen» (480; the casual chat-message question uses the close form «¿me haces un resumen…?»). «acortar texto» (70) is low volume but is literally what the extension does, so it carries "shorten". «parafrasear» (rephrase) and the competitor «resoomer» are not used. Because the name is also the toolbar icon's title, step 1 points at the icon «Resumidor de textos». Everywhere else the product stays Make It Shorter.
The misspelling question uses «resúmen» (70/month), the common wrong accent on the singular «resumen».

## Short description
Resumidor de textos con IA que acorta una frase o un artículo entero. Pega lo que sea y redúcelo con un clic, en cualquier idioma.
An AI text summarizer that shortens a single sentence or a whole article. Paste anything and cut it down with one click, in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: "en un clic" → "con un clic".
- Opening: the calque "comes back to you as the same text" → "give it an article… and it returns the same text".
- Step 1: "the icon Resumidor de textos" → "the icon of Resumidor de textos".
- "A short paragraph keeps all its points" → "all its ideas".
- Language setting: the bare infinitives "summarize texts and translate them" read as keyword stuffing → "summarizes and translates at once".
- Uses: "seminar" → "class"; "this thread" (nothing for "this" to point to) → "the thread"; the research line reworded as "a first sift through your bibliography"; "run Make It Shorter through five sources" turned the right way round; the editor's request now names the text.
- "Worth twenty minutes" and "which part holds the answer" rephrased; "AI works underneath" (an "under the hood" calque) → "it runs on AI".
- Limits: "each time" → "per summary"; "Chrome's own pages" → "Chrome's internal pages"; article added before Chrome Web Store.
- FAQ: "short paragraph or very short" redundancy fixed; "pictures of text" → "the text inside images"; "your text" → "my text" (the reader is asking); "retold version" → "paraphrase".
- Misspelling question: the reader found it SEO-ish and condescending and suggested cutting it. Kept per the course, but without the spelling lesson: "I typed «resúmen» with an accent by mistake. Is it the same? Yes, it's a very common slip."
- Not from the blind read: "reload the whole page" (Chrome's own name for refresh) → "bring the whole page's text back into the panel".
- Closing line: three stacked verbs trimmed, and "see what a page says" → "find out what a page is about".
