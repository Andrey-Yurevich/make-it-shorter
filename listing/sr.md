## Blind read
- "get back as the same text" calque → "becomes the same text"; "with nothing added" reworded more naturally.
- "source" (извор) → "original" (оригинал) throughout; "check the original" when a detail is decisive.
- Repeated verb in "a bit shorter or a lot shorter" merged into one.
- "in one click" → the idiomatic instrumental form "with one click".
- Study, work and research examples reworded: "condense into short notes", "boil it down to the essentials", research line no longer mixes a noun with an imperative.
- "pastes neatly" calque → "fits nicely into"; "deserves twenty minutes" → "is worth twenty minutes of reading".
- FAQ: "key points" → "only what matters"; "fit into a chat message" → "fit into one message"; "which language does the summary arrive in" → "which language will the summary be in"; "reads words, not pictures of them" → "reads text on the page, not text in images"; "you're ready" → "that's all".
- Latin-script answer (replaces the summerize misspelling question) no longer reads as a keyword insert: "whichever script you type in, you reach the same tool".
- Closing line: "before you commit to reading the whole page" made grammatical.
