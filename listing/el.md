# Greek (el)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter (brand, unchanged)
Not a priority market (Semrush database `none`, Semrush skipped). Wording comes from common Greek usage: «περίληψη κειμένου» ("text summary"), «κάντε περίληψη» ("summarize"), «συντομέψτε / κόψτε το» ("shorten / cut it down"), «περίληψη με AI». The misspelling FAQ (summerize) becomes accentless typing, «περιληψη κειμενου» with no accents. Greeks really do search that way.
The names of Chrome's parts (γραμμή εργαλείων "toolbar", πλαϊνό πάνελ "side panel", καρφιτσώστε "pin", πρόγραμμα προβολής PDF "PDF viewer") come from general knowledge. They were not checked against Chrome's own Greek strings: neither Chrome's files nor its help pages were reachable from this session. Chrome may call the side panel «πλαϊνό πλαίσιο».
