# Norwegian (Bokmål) (no)

## Name
Oppsummer tekst — Summarize text
Priority market. Semrush, database `no` (Wordstat not applicable; Semrush used throughout): `make it shorter` returned no data, i.e. 0 measurable volume; best national phrasing „oppsummering av tekst“ 20 (×10 = 200). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. The measured phrase is the noun form ("summary of text"); the name uses the imperative of the same verb, „Oppsummer tekst“, because it reads as a tool name and lets the short description open with a real sentence instead of a label (same choice as Swedish „Sammanfatta text“). The bare nouns „oppsummering“ (1,900, merged with its misspellings) and „sammendrag“ (480, mostly crossword traffic) are too broad to be a name; „skrive sammendrag“ (40) is people asking how to write one, not looking for a tool; „summarize“ (390) is English. No AI compound and no "shorten text" phrasing („forkorte tekst“, „korte ned tekst“) has measurable volume, so they are just ordinary wording. Since the name is also the toolbar icon's title, step 1 points at the icon Oppsummer tekst; elsewhere the product stays Make It Shorter.
The misspelling FAQ uses „oppsumere“ / „oppsumering“ (single m). „oppsumere“ shows 20 on its own; „oppsumering“ shows numbers identical to the correct spelling, i.e. merged into it.
Form of address: „du“ throughout. "AI" is kept rather than the official „KI“: it is what Norwegian product pages commonly say.

## Short description
Oppsummer tekst med ett klikk. Lim inn alt fra én setning til hele artikler, så korter AI-en det ned på valgfritt språk.
Summarize text with one click. Paste anything from a single sentence to whole articles, and the AI shortens it in any language you choose.

## Blind read
- Short description: bare "AI" as subject read like a headline → „AI-en“.
- "Document with sections": „avsnitt“ means paragraph in Norwegian → "a document with subheadings keeps its structure".
- "Nothing is made up" tense fixed („blir diktet opp“); "shorten it a bit or a lot" reworded without the doubled „ned“.
- Settings heading: "you only need to choose once"; language line without the doubled „hvilket“.
- Research use: reworded as "a quick first overview of the reading list … including papers on artificial intelligence"; „leselista“ → „leselisten“ to match the -en forms.
- Caveat: "worth twenty minutes of your time, and where the answer is"; "There is AI behind it, so nuances may get lost".
- Limits heading grammar fixed; PDF viewer → „PDF-leseren“.
- FAQ: Simplified "er laget for"; chat answer made concrete ("down to a small paragraph or even less, run the text through once more"); image question asks about the extension, not "you"; misspelling question uses „ved en feiltakelse“; draft answer "like text from any page".
- Closing line: anglicism „er for når“ → „passer når“, "read the whole page".
- Declined: dropping the misspelling FAQ (the reader flagged it as keyword stuffing, but the course keeps it); switching "AI" to „KI“.
