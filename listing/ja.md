# Japanese (ja)

## Name
AI文章要約 — AI text summary
Priority market. Semrush, database `jp`: `make it shorter` gets 30 searches a month. The best national phrasing is the AI-summary cluster: `AI要約` / `要約AI` / `ai 要約` / `要約 ai` all show the identical 5,400, so it is one figure, not four. 5,400 ×10 = 54,000, far more than 10× ahead. Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone.
Bare `要約` (22,200) and bare `AI要約` are not used as the name. The related queries for `要約` are school questions (要約とは, 書き方, コツ), and `AI要約` is shared with PDF, meeting-minutes, audio and YouTube summarizers. So the name adds 文章 ("text"), which also carries `文章要約` (1,300) and `文章要約ai` (720). The cost is that the name doesn't contain `AI要約` as one unbroken string. Whether Chrome Web Store search still matches it can't be checked from here.
Secondary keywords: `論文 要約 ai` (1,000) is used for "summarize ai papers". `記事 要約` gets only 50, so "article summarizer" is plain prose. The shortening phrases (make it short, way shorter, as short as possible…) use 短く / 簡潔に instead of repeating 要約. The name is the toolbar icon's title, so step 1 points at the icon 「AI文章要約」. The opening line ties the two names together as Make It Shorter（AI文章要約）, and the product is Make It Shorter everywhere else.
Misspelling FAQ: `summerize` becomes `要訳`, a wrong-kanji spelling of 要約 that Semrush shows at 170 a month. I left out the hiragana `ようやく ai` (5,400): that is the same Semrush cluster, and the word itself means "finally".
Chrome's Japanese terms (ツールバー, サイドパネル, 固定, 拡張機能, Chrome ウェブストア) come from memory and were not checked against Chrome's own strings.

## Short description
AI文章要約で、短い文から長い記事までワンクリックで簡潔に。テキストを貼り付けるだけで、どの言語でもすっきりまとまります。
With AI text summary, anything from a short sentence to a long article becomes concise in one click. Just paste your text and it is neatly condensed in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: 一文 → 短い文. Summarizing "a single sentence" sounded odd. That put 短 in the line twice, so the ending changed from 短くまとめられます to すっきりまとまります ("neatly condensed").
- Opening: "comes back shorter" calque → "condenses it, keeping the content". The "pick language and tone, nothing added" sentence was reworded as suggested.
- Step 4: "watch it being written" → "the result appears in real time".
- "Plain summarizer" → "typical summarizer" (ふつう sounded too casual). The images line and the "nothing made up" line were reworded (作り話 sounded odd for a tool).
- Settings heading, "receive a summary" calque, 標準 → 初期設定 (the usual word for "default").
- Use cases 1, 3 and 4 reworded: 3 had a translated-sounding "first pass through a reading list"; 4 is now "find the one with the answer".
- Limits: the heading is now "Notes on use", "requests" became "uses per day", and "built-in PDF viewer" became "a PDF opened in Chrome" (how Japanese users say it).
- FAQ: instructions end in ください; the broken chat-message answer was rebuilt; "rewrite or shorten" is now asked as "a rewriting tool or a shortening tool". The calqued "does what 'make it shorter please' asks" was dropped, keeping its meaning: the same message in fewer words. The drafts answer now compares with "web pages".
- ぐっと ("way shorter") was cut from four uses to one. The closing line still has both "way shorter" (大幅に) and "as short as possible" (できるかぎり), because the source has both.
- Name link: the opening line is now Make It Shorter（AI文章要約）.
- Declined: deleting the 要訳 FAQ. The reader thought it looked like keyword bait and noted that 要訳 is also a rare real word ("gist translation"). It is the native counterpart of the summerize line, so it was rephrased instead: "I searched for 要訳" and "people often write 要約 as 要訳". It no longer says 要訳 isn't a word. Also declined: merging Research and Looking things up. They are separate items in the source.
