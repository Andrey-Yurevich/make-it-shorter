## Short description
AI ช่วยย่อประโยคและสรุปเนื้อหายาวทั้งบท แค่วางข้อความแล้วคลิกครั้งเดียว ก็ได้ฉบับที่สั้นลงเป็นภาษาที่คุณต้องการ
AI shortens sentences and summarizes whole long texts. Just paste the text and click once to get a shorter version in whatever language you want.

## Blind read
- Intro: the "more to read than time" idiom is now said the Thai way ("a lot to read, little time"), and the "same text, only shorter" sentence was rebuilt from a chain of fragments.
- "Select" became ไฮไลต์ (highlight), the word Thai users use for selecting text on a page. Changed in step 2 and in the FAQ.
- Step 3 word order fixed ("language and tone of the result"). Step 4 no longer reads as if the result writes itself.
- "In one click" changed to native forms (แค่คลิกเดียว, ด้วยคลิกเดียว, คลิกครั้งเดียว).
- Lists/tables bullets: a calque of "lists stay lists" became the natural "point-form content stays in points". The translation tell ของมัน ("its") was removed.
- Uses: "seminar" became "class". "This thread" became "long threads". The AI-papers clause is now a sentence of its own. "Looking things up" was renamed so it no longer duplicates "Research". The stiff ต้นร่าง became ดราฟต์ ("draft").
- The PDF/Chrome limit line was rebuilt so the verb comes first. It now says "PDF files opened in Chrome" instead of naming a viewer.
- FAQ: the colloquial มั้ย became ไหม to match the other questions. The chat answer was reordered so the "if" is unambiguous. The image answer says "can't read text inside images". The quoted "make it shorter please" was dropped as forced. The meaning ("shortens as asked, same content in fewer words, not a retelling") is kept.
- Closing line: the "for when…" calque and the triple repetition of "shorten" were removed.
- Not a blind-read change: the summerize/summerizer misspelling Q&A was dropped while drafting, because Thai has no common equivalent.
