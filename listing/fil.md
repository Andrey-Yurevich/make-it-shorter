# Filipino (fil)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter (brand name, kept in English)
not a priority market

## Short description
Paikliin gamit ang AI ang kahit anong pangungusap o buong teksto. I-paste lang, at sa isang click may buod ka na, sa anumang wika.
Shorten any sentence or a whole text with AI. Just paste it, and in one click you have a summary, in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: "the sentence" became "any sentence", so it no longer reads as one specific sentence.
- Intro: the English "more to read than time" construction became the native "a lot to read but short on time", and "comes back as" became "is returned to you".
- "pinili" (chose) became "na-highlight" (highlighted), both in the steps and in the FAQ.
- "Plain summarizer" heading: the made-up noun "pambuod" became "karaniwang tool sa pagbubuod" (a usual summarizing tool).
- "How much shorter" paragraph reworded into native word order. "Bahagyang paikli" used a verb root as a noun and was fixed.
- Settings: "Makuha…" (an odd command) became "Makakakuha ka…" (you get…), and the tone sentence was reworded.
- Use cases: "seminar" became "klase" (class). The research item, a fragment, became a full sentence. "Itira" became "itabi lang" (keep just).
- Caveats paragraph: calques of "the summary tells you" and "when a detail decides" were reworded, and a double hedge was removed.
- Limits: added "pero" (but), "bawat beses" became "kada request" and "Chrome's own pages" became "internal na page ng Chrome".
- FAQ: "run it again" became "i-Shorten mo ulit". The default-language answer was reworded. The screenshots answer was fixed: it had said "not pictures of the words".
- Closing line: the three near-identical "shorten" clauses read as stuffing and were merged into "a shorter or the shortest version".
- Not taken: replacing "pambuod" with the English "text summarizer" (English search phrases are not left in place), and an added "(gaya ng Settings)" example (a new claim).
- Misspelling Q&A (summerize) dropped: Filipino has no common misspelling of "buod"/"ibuod" to put in its place.
