# Arabic (ar)

## Name
تلخيص النصوص — Text summarization
Semrush `sa`: `make it shorter` has no data (0). Best national phrasing تلخيص النصوص / تلخيص نص 210 × 10 = 2,100 → national 10×+ ahead → national phrasing alone. Generic تلخيص (1,600) and اختصار (8,100, "abbreviation") rejected: school-exercise and unrelated queries. Other keywords used: تقليص نص (170, "shrink text"), لخصلي (210, colloquial "summarize it for me"). The misspelling Q&A (summerize) became one about لخصلي written joined (210) instead of the standard لخص لي (50). Step 1 of "How it works" names the icon تلخيص النصوص, as the Arabic toolbar shows it.

## Short description
تلخيص النصوص بالذكاء الاصطناعي بنقرة واحدة. الصق جملة أو مقالًا كاملًا لتحصل على نسخة أقصر منه، وبأي لغة تريد.
AI text summarization in one click. Paste a sentence or a whole article to get a shorter version of it, in any language you like.

- "Pull in the whole page again" no longer reads as refreshing the browser page.

- «سواء… أو» fixed to «سواء… أم».
- "Forward" → Saudi «تحوّلها».
- Research/lookup items: calques "run through" and the clumsy research sentence rewritten as imperatives, meaning kept.
- Smaller rewordings: "a summary tells you", "each pass", "Simplified is there to", "make it shorter please", closing line.
