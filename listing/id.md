# Indonesian (id)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter
Not a priority market (Semrush database `none`, so Semrush was skipped). Wording comes from general knowledge of Indonesian usage. The main verbs are "meringkas / ringkasan" (summarize / summary) and "mempersingkat" (shorten), with "merangkum / rangkuman" used once or twice and "singkat, padat, dan jelas" (short, concise and clear) for "short and to the point". Address is the formal "Anda" throughout. Numbers use Indonesian separators (30.000). "Toolbar" is used on the assumption that it matches Chrome's Indonesian UI. This is not verified, because web search was not permitted here. If it is wrong, it is a one-word fix.
Misspelling FAQ: Indonesian has no common misspelling of "summarize". It was replaced with the real variant pair "rangkuman" vs "ringkasan" (two everyday words for "summary" that people search both ways).

## Short description
Persingkat kalimat atau seluruh tulisan dan buat ringkasannya dengan AI. Tempel teks, sekali klik, siap dalam bahasa apa pun.
Shorten a sentence or a whole piece of writing and make a summary of it with AI. Paste text, one click, ready in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: the clipped "result in any language" became "ready in any language" (the full "get the result…" was over 132 characters). "klik sekali" was changed to the more idiomatic "sekali klik".
- Opening: the calque "more reading than time to read it" became "your reading piles up while your time is limited". "AI summarizer" became "AI-based summarizing tool". "Comes back as" became "is turned into".
- Step 2 grammar fixed ("the whole article will load"). Step 4: "watch the result being written" became "see the result appear live".
- Pictures "next to" their paragraphs became "accompany" them. "Shrinks" became "is trimmed down to". "Loses extra words" became "drops unnecessary words".
- Default tone: the too-casual "Bawaannya" became "Pilihan awalnya" (the initial choice).
- "Seminar" (a conference in Indonesia) became "kuliah" (lecture). The thread use case lost the odd "this" and gained an object ("a short, concise, clear version"). The research line now uses the verb, "summarize articles…, including papers about AI", instead of the stuffed noun "article summarizer".
- "Keep the one that answers" became "pick the one". The "deserves twenty minutes", "when a detail decides" and "AI underneath" sentences were reworded idiomatically ("after all, it's still AI").
- Limits: "Summarizing is free" became "Free". "There" became "for pages like these".
- FAQ: "each round" became "each time you repeat it". The Simplified answer was smoothed. "Very short" became "even more concise". The language answer became a conditional ("if you pick another language…"). "Pictures of words" became "text inside images". "Retelling" was rephrased as a verb. "Is it the same?" became "Is it all the same?". "Summary generator" became "use it to make a summary of my drafts". "Ready" became "can use it right away".
- Closing: the calque "for when you need" became "is right for when you need".
- Declined: "parafrase" for "retelling" (it would claim the product rewrites text) and "long email thread" (it adds "long" and "email").
