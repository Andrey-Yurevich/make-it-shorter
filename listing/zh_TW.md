# Traditional Chinese (zh_TW)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter
not a priority market

## Short description
用 AI 縮短句子、為整篇文章產生摘要。貼上文字，一鍵完成，任何語言都適用。
Use AI to shorten sentences and summarize whole articles. Paste the text, done in one click, works in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: "condense one sentence" (odd) became "shorten sentences".
- Opening: "and adds nothing" (calque) became "and won't add any content".
- Step 4: "watch the result being written" became "the result appears word by word, live".
- "Reload the whole page" became "re-read the whole page's content". In zh-TW Chrome, 重新載入 means the browser's Reload.
- Lists and tables: the "list stays list" calque was reworded natively.
- Work: "the whole thread" became "the whole email chain". "Straight to the point" was simplified.
- Research and Looking things up: the "first pass through a reading list" and "five sources" calques were recast as a question plus an answer. "AI papers" was kept: the reader's version read as "papers summarized by AI", which changes the claim.
- "Pastes neatly": the reader's "formatting won't break" was declined because it claims more than the source. It became "comes out clean and tidy".
- Smaller wording fixes: "result done" became "once the result is out". The reader's "summary done" was declined because it adds a keyword the source does not have. "Decisive detail", "AI underneath", "made for this" and "pictures of text" were reworded.
- Limits: "only the first part is shortened" became "only the first part is processed". This avoids a reading where the rest is kept unshortened.
- The "make it shorter please" FAQ was rephrased. "Retelling" became "saying it again in other words".
- Misspelling FAQ: summerize was replaced with 精減, a common homophone typo for 精簡 ("condense") in Zhuyin input. The question was softened to "will I find the same tool?". The answer was kept.
- Closing line: the run-on list was recast as "whether you want X, Y or Z, Make It Shorter has it covered".
- Declined: glossing Simplified as 「Simplified（淺白）」. The reader noted that "Simplified" can suggest Simplified Chinese. But the label stays exactly as on screen, and the sentence right after it already says "plain words".
