# Slovak (sk)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter
not a priority market

## Short description
Pomocou AI skráti a zhrnie čokoľvek od jednej vety po celý článok, v akomkoľvek jazyku. Stačí vložiť text a raz kliknúť.
Using AI, it shortens and summarizes anything from a single sentence to a whole article, in any language. Just paste the text and click once.

## Blind read
- Short description: "shortens and summarizes one sentence and a whole article" → "anything from one sentence to a whole article", because Slovak does not summarize a single sentence.
- "with nothing extra" (calque) → "with no added information"; "watch the result being written" → "watch the result appear step by step".
- Sections line: dropped the repeated "parts" ("a document split into sections keeps them"). Pictures line → "stay next to the paragraphs they belong to".
- "depends on the source" → "depends on what the original is like"; "keeps every point" → "keeps all the information" ("body" read as list items); fixed an unclear pronoun in "a bit shorter or a lot shorter".
- Removed "language" twice in one sentence; heading "What it's good for every day" → "Where it helps you every day".
- Research use: changed a noun phrase among imperatives to an imperative, and the calque "first pass" → "first going through the literature". "Keep the one" → a more natural verb.
- "Copied text pastes neatly" → "you paste copied text without trouble". Not taken: the reader's "keeps its formatting", which claims more than the source does.
- "Behind it all is AI" (read as conspiratorial) → "the text is processed by AI, so it may occasionally miss a nuance".
- Daily limit: "requests per device" (technical) → "number of shortenings per device per day".
- "Chrome's own pages" (calque) → "Chrome system pages"; "PDF viewer" → the more common Slovak word.
- FAQ: "one click on Shorten" → "just click Shorten"; "reads words, not pictures of them" → "reads text on the page, not text in images"; "fewer words" phrasing; "your choice is remembered next time" → "the extension remembers your choice".
- Closing line: dropped the repeated "shorten… shorten" and the awkward "commit to reading in full".
- The misspelling question (summerize/summerizer) was dropped before the read. Slovak has no common misspelling of the product word: typing without diacritics is a keyboard habit, and no Slovak page would carry that question. That leaves 9 Q&A pairs instead of 10.
