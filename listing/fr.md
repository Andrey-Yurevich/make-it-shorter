# French (fr)

## Name
Résumer un texte — Summarize a text
Priority market. Semrush, database `fr`: `make it shorter` 30/month; best national phrasing «résumer un texte» 390 (×10 = 3,900, ~130× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. The infinitive beats the noun «résumé de texte» (320) because its cluster is larger: «résumer un texte» 390 + unaccented «resumer un texte» 320 + misspelled «résumé un texte» 390, about 1,100 in all, against about 430 for the noun forms. «raccourcir un texte» ("shorten a text") gets 90 and is used as the secondary keyword. «reformuler un texte» (14,800) means "rephrase", which the extension does not do, so it appears only as the verb in the FAQ question «Cet outil reformule-t-il votre texte… ?» ("Does this tool rewrite your text…?"). The competitor brand «resoomer» (1,000) is not used anywhere.
The misspelling FAQ uses «résumé un texte» (390), the very common confusion of é and -er, and adds that people often leave the accent off; unaccented «resumer» (720) is not written out.

## Short description
Résumer un texte ou raccourcir une phrase avec l'IA, dans la langue de votre choix. Collez un article entier, un seul clic suffit.
Summarize a text or shorten a sentence with AI, in the language of your choice. Paste a whole article, a single click is enough.
