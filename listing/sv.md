# Swedish (sv)

## Name
Sammanfatta text — Summarize text
Priority market. Semrush, database `se`: `make it shorter` 40/month; best national phrasing „sammanfatta text“ 210 (×10 = 2,100, about 52× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. „sammanfatta texter“ shows the identical 210/CPC/competition, so Semrush folds it into the same cluster; it is not a second keyword. The noun „sammanfattning“ (3,600, "summary") and the bare „förkorta“ (170, "shorten") are too broad to be a name. Secondary keywords: „förkorta text“ and „korta ner text“ (30 each, literal "shorten text") and „sammanfattning“ as the ordinary noun. No AI compound („AI-sammanfattning“, „sammanfatta med AI“) has measurable volume, so "AI" is used as a plain word. „sammanfatta engelska“ (1,600) is people looking up the English word, not a tool, and is ignored. Since the name is also the toolbar icon's title, step 1 points at the icon Sammanfatta text; elsewhere the product stays Make It Shorter.
The misspelling FAQ uses „sammanfatning“ / „sammanfata“ (single t), a common Swedish slip. Semrush reports 3,600 for „sammanfatning“, identical to the correct spelling, i.e. the typo is merged into it, not measured on its own.
Form of address: „du“ throughout, the norm in Swedish store listings.

## Short description
Sammanfatta text med ett enda klick. Klistra in meningar eller hela artiklar, så kortar AI ner dem på valfritt språk.
Summarize text with a single click. Paste sentences or whole articles, and AI shortens them in any language you like.

## Blind read
- Short description: "the whole article" (definite, nothing to refer to) → plural "sentences or whole articles" (also avoids repeating a word).
- Opening: "more to read than time to read" repetition → "more to read than you have time for"; "comes back as the same text" calque → "whether it is … you get back the same text".
- List: "each one shorter" calque → "only shorter"; pictures "stay by the right paragraph"; "key ideas" → "boils down to the essentials".
- Settings: "choose once" → "only need to choose once"; imperative "Get your summary" → "you get the summary in whichever of 57 languages".
- Uses: notes line smoothed; the AI-papers clause reworded ("even those about AI"); "pastes neatly" („prydlig“ read oddly) → "pastes smoothly"; "Faktakoll" (fact-checking) → "Söka svar" (looking for answers), closer to the English "looking things up".
- Caveat: "worth twenty minutes" → "worth spending twenty minutes on"; "when a detail is decisive … you should go back to the source".
- Limits: daily limit rephrased ("the number of requests per device and day is limited"); "cannot be read" → "the extension cannot read".
- FAQ: "still long" → "still too long"; "pass" → "round"; Simplified answer word order; chat question gets a clear subject ("the text"); its answer made readable; "It shortens." → "It shortens it."; "retelling" → "rewrite"; "you've landed right" → "you've come to the right place".
- Closing line: keyword-list shape → "when a text needs to get much shorter, ideally as short as possible".
