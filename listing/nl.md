## Name
Samenvatting maken — Make a summary
Priority market. Semrush, database `nl`: `make it shorter` 20/month; best national phrasing „samenvatting maken“ 480 (×10 = 4,800, 240× ahead). Rule: national 10× or more ahead → national phrasing alone. The bare „samenvatten“ / „samenvattingen“ (590 each) are too broad to be a name. Secondary keywords: „samenvatten“ (590), „AI-samenvatter“ (`ai samenvatter` 110) and „AI-samenvatting“ (`samenvatting ai` 170); "summary generator" in the drafts question is rendered with the name keyword („een samenvatting … maken“) rather than the split query „samenvatting maker“ (390), which would read as a spelling error in prose. „tekst inkorten“ / „tekst korter maken“ (20 each) are literal "shorten text" and are used as plain verbs, not as keywords. Since the name is also the toolbar icon's title, step 1 points at the icon of Samenvatting maken; elsewhere the product stays Make It Shorter.
The misspelling FAQ uses „samenvating“ (210/month) and „samenvattig“ (140/month), both real typos for „samenvatting“.
Form of address: informal „je“ throughout, the norm in Dutch store listings.
