# Ukrainian (uk)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter
Not a priority market.

## Short description
ШІ скорочує речення чи цілу статтю та виокремлює головне. Вставте текст — і за один клік отримаєте результат будь-якою мовою.
AI shortens a sentence or a whole article and pulls out the key points. Paste text and get the result in one click, in any language.

Note: the misspelling FAQ (summerize/summerizer) became a question about «саммарі» / «самарі», two spellings of the loanword for "summary" seen in Ukrainian; it treats them as variants, not as a mistake.

## Blind read
- Short description: "summarizes the main points" became "pulls out the main points" (the verb now fits its object).
- Opening: "comes back as the same text" recast as "the output is the same text, only shorter".
- "A plain summary service" heading became "ordinary text-shortening services", which reads less bureaucratic.
- Pictures line: "stand next to" became "stay next to"; the sections line was reworded so the two lines no longer repeat a verb.
- "Settings you choose once" became "settings it is enough to choose once".
- Study use: "shorten to a minimum for notes" became "shorten to concise notes"; "email thread" took the common Ukrainian term.
- "Pastes neatly" became "easy to paste". The reader's "without extra formatting" was declined because it adds a claim.
- "Deserves twenty minutes", "which part holds the answer" and "when a detail decides something" reworded to sound natural; "source" became "original".
- Limits: the unreadable-pages line now names the extension as the subject.
- FAQ: "only a few lines", "reads text on the page, not in pictures", "does what you mean when you ask 'shorten it, please'", and the misspelling answer now ends with "that is exactly what Make It Shorter does"; the drafts question was rephrased.
- Kept despite the note: "even AI papers" in the research use, because it is part of the meaning.
