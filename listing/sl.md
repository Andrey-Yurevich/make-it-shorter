## Short description
Z umetno inteligenco skrajšajte stavek ali cel članek. Prilepite besedilo in z enim klikom dobite povzetek v poljubnem jeziku.
Shorten a sentence or a whole article with artificial intelligence. Paste your text and get a summary in any language with one click.

## Blind read
- Opening: "more to read than time for it" and "you get it back as the same text" read as calques; now "who have to read more than they have time for" and "turns into the same text, just shorter".
- The feature list heading, the list item about points, and the table item were reworded (the table item also lost a stray comma). "Ločen prevajalnik" became "poseben prevajalnik", and "Privzeti je" became "Privzeti ton je".
- The everyday-uses heading is now "for everyday use". The study item is now "shorten it into short notes". The research item was a noun phrase and is now an imperative like the other items; the AI-papers point stays.
- "Separate window" became "new window". The copy line is now "you can paste it without trouble". The twenty-minutes and contract sentences were reworded. "A nuance may slip" now clearly means slipping past the AI, not past the reader.
- Limits: the heading is now "what is good to know about the limits", the free-with-daily-limit line was reworded, and "Chrome's own pages" became "Chrome's internal pages".
- FAQ questions now start with "Ali lahko …" (the written form). Several answers were tightened: "each pass makes it much shorter, until only key points are left", "only a few words", and "reads the text on a page, not text in images".
- "Prepiše" can mean "copies", so it became "napiše na novo" ("rewrites").
- Misspelling FAQ: kept as the Slovenian equivalent, i.e. typing without č/š/ž. The question now uses one example ("skrajsaj besedilo") instead of two, because the reader found it keyword-stuffed, and the answer was split into two sentences.
- "Summary generator for my drafts" read as a translated keyword; now "can I use it to summarize my own drafts".
- Closing sentence: "what a page says" became "what a page is about", and "before you take on a full read" became "before you read it in full".
