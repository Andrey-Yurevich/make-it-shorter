# Bulgarian (bg)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter
not a priority market

## Short description
Съкратете изречение или цяла статия чрез изкуствен интелект. Поставете текста и получете обобщение на желания език с едно кликване.
Shorten a sentence or a whole article with artificial intelligence. Paste the text and get a summary in the language you want, in one click.

## Blind read
- Short description: "in every language" (read as all at once) → "in the desired language".
- Intro: calqued "more to read than time to read it" → "a lot to read and little time"; "nothing added" made a verb phrase.
- Everyday uses: "make it very short" redundancy → "boil it down to a few lines"; "run Make It Shorter on" calque → "shorten five sources with Make It Shorter"; research line reworded.
- "pastes neatly", "deserves twenty minutes", "AI underneath", "Chrome's own pages", "each pass" calques → native phrasing; two stiff passives made active.
- FAQ: chat question reworded, "account" → "registration", "and you are ready" → „и готово“ ("and done"), "make it shorter please" → „Съкрати го, моля“ ("shorten it, please"), comma fix in the variant answer, closing line made one subject.

Note: the summerize/summerizer question has no Bulgarian equivalent; it became the same point about the two common words for a summary, резюме / обобщение.
