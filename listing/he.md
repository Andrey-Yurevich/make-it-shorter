# Hebrew (he)

## Name
Make It Shorter — Make It Shorter (brand kept in Latin letters)
not a priority market
The misspelling FAQ (summerize) was dropped: Hebrew has no common misspelling of "summarize" that people search for.
Chrome UI terms (toolbar סרגל הכלים, side panel חלונית צדדית, Chrome Web Store חנות האינטרנט של Chrome, PDF viewer מציג ה-PDF) were not checked online: web access was declined, so these are Chrome's Hebrew names as I know them.

## Short description
כלי בינה מלאכותית שמקצר משפט בודד ומסכם מאמר שלם. הדביקו טקסט, ובלחיצה אחת תקבלו גרסה תמציתית בכל שפה.
An AI tool that shortens a single sentence and summarizes a whole article. Paste text, and in one click you get a concise version in any language.

## Blind read
- Short description: "AI that shortens…" became "an AI tool that shortens…"; "click once in order to get" became "in one click you'll get".
- Opening: the English "more to read than time to read" shape became "a lot to read and little time".
- "A document with sections keeps its sections" now reads "a document with chapters keeps the same chapter division".
- Study: "seminar" became "class" (the usual Hebrew word), and the calque "really short" became "to the minimum".
- Work: "turn it short and to the point" became "shorten it to the essentials only".
- Research: "first round" became the idiomatic "first pass". The reader suggested dropping the AI-papers point, but it was kept because the source has it.
- Editor: "an editor asks if…" became "the editor asked to trim a bit?"
- "A summary will tell you whether an article is worth twenty minutes" was recast as "from the summary you'll know whether it's worth investing twenty minutes"; "when a detail is decisive" became "when a specific detail matters… check the source".
- Limits: the reflexive "shortens itself" became the passive "is shortened"; the PDF-viewer sentence now has normal subject-first order.
- FAQ: "get it short and simple" became "get a short, simple version"; "the size of a chat message" became "the length of a chat message"; the ambiguous "run it once more" became "shorten the result again"; the image answer is clearer; "a summary generator for my drafts" (flagged as SEO) became "can I summarize my own drafts with it too", and drafts are summarized like "any other text" rather than "any other page".
- Closing: "commit to reading" calque became "invest time in reading all of it"; "shorten a text by a lot" became "significantly".
