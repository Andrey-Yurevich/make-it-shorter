# European Portuguese (pt_PT)

## Name
Resumir texto — Summarize text
Priority market. Semrush database `pt`: `make it shorter` has no data (below the reporting floor). The best national phrasing is “resumir texto” at 590/month. Multiplied by 10 that is 5,900, so the national phrasing is 10× or more ahead, and the rule gives the national phrasing alone. It is written in sentence case, the same as pt_BR.
Secondary keywords:
- “resumidor de texto(s)” at 320 renders "summarizer". It is used once, in the opening line. The blind reader found it uncommon in Portugal.
- “resumo de texto” at 320 and “resumo” at 2,400 render "summary".
- “gerador de resumo” at 40 is used in the drafts question.
- Plain "shorten" is rendered with “encurtar”/“reduzir”. “encurtar texto” and “reduzir texto” get only 20 each.
- “Resoomer” at 590 is a competitor brand and is left out.

The name is also the toolbar icon's title, so step 1 points at the icon «Resumir texto». Everywhere else the product stays Make It Shorter.

For the misspelling FAQ: “rezumir”, “rezumidor” and “resomir” have no data. “rezumo” shows 2,400, an exact copy of “resumo”, so it is most likely Semrush folding the two together, not a real typo. The question was therefore remapped to “sumarizar” (20/month), the anglicism Portuguese speakers use for "summarize". It plays the same role as summerize does in English.

## Short description
Resumir texto com IA está à distância de um clique. Cole frases ou artigos inteiros e receba-os mais curtos, em qualquer língua.
Summarizing text with AI is just one click away. Paste sentences or whole articles and get them back shorter, in any language.
